        About

        I'm Eliana. I study Industrial and Interaction Design at Syracuse University, and I graduate in 2027.

        To me, design is storytelling. A product tells its story through its shape, the feel of its material, and the way it fits into someone's day. My favorite moment is when an idea stops being a drawing and becomes something you can hold, test, and argue with.

        I sketch fast, build rough, and put things in front of real people early. Then I refine until the object feels obvious, like it couldn't have been any other way.
